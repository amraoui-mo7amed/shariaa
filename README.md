# Shariaa Studies Curriculum (العلوم الشرعية)

A structured collection of Islamic studies texts, audio lessons, explanations, and summaries, organized sequentially with standardized English directory and file names.

## 📚 Curriculum Structure

| # | Subject Directory | Arabic Title | Description | Lessons |
|---|-------------------|--------------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | `01_Three_Principles_Al-Usul_Al-Thalatha` | الأصول الثلاثة وأدلتها | The Three Fundamental Principles by Sheikh Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab | 16 Lessons + Summaries & Booklets |
| 2 | `02_Four_Principles_Al-Qawaid_Al-Arbaa` | القواعد الأربعة | The Four Foundations | 4 Lessons |
| 3 | `03_Conditions_of_Prayer_Shurut_Al-Salah` | شروط الصلاة وأركانها وواجباتها | The Conditions, Pillars, and Requirements of Prayer | 9 Lessons |
| 4 | `04_Prophets_Biography_Mukhtasar_Al-Seerah` | مختصر سيرة الرسول ﷺ | Concise Biography of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ | 3 Lessons |
| 5 | `05_Names_of_Allah_Tafseer_Al-Asmaa_Al-Husna` | تفسير أسماء الله الحسنى | Explanation and Meanings of the Most Beautiful Names of Allah | 12 Lessons |
| 6 | `06_Sunnah_Creed_Aqeedat_Ahl_Al-Sunnah` | عقيدة أهل السنة والجماعة | The Creed of Ahlus-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah | 34 Lessons |
| 7 | `07_Book_of_Tawheed_Kitab_At-Tawheed` | كتاب التوحيد | Kitab At-Tawheed (The Book of Monotheism) | 24 Lessons |

## 🗂 Directory & File Organization

Each subject directory follows a clean, standardized structure:
- `Audio/`: Lesson audio recordings formatted as `Lesson_01.mp3`, `Lesson_02.mp3`, etc.
- `PDF/`: Study materials, lesson texts, transcripts formatted as `Lesson_01.pdf`, `Lesson_02.pdf`, along with weekly summaries (`Summary_Week_01.pdf`).
