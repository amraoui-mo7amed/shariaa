# Shariaa Studies Curriculum (العلوم الشرعية)

A structured collection of Islamic studies texts, audio lessons, explanations, and summaries, organized sequentially.

## 📚 Curriculum Structure

| # | Folder Name | Arabic Title | Description |
|---|-------------|--------------|-------------|
| 1 | `01_Three_Principles_Al-Usul_Al-Thalatha` | الأصول الثلاثة وأدلتها | The Three Fundamental Principles by Sheikh Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab |
| 2 | `02_Four_Principles_Al-Qawaid_Al-Arbaa` | القواعد الأربعة | The Four Foundations |
| 3 | `03_Conditions_of_Prayer_Shurut_Al-Salah` | شروط الصلاة وأركانها وواجباتها | The Conditions, Pillars, and Requirements of Prayer |
| 4 | `04_Prophets_Biography_Mukhtasar_Al-Seerah` | مختصر سيرة الرسول ﷺ | Concise Biography of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ |
| 5 | `05_Names_of_Allah_Tafseer_Al-Asmaa_Al-Husna` | تفسير أسماء الله الحسنى | Explanation and Meanings of the Most Beautiful Names of Allah |
| 6 | `06_Sunnah_Creed_Aqeedat_Ahl_Al-Sunnah` | عقيدة أهل السنة والجماعة | The Creed of Ahlus-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah |
| 7 | `07_Book_of_Tawheed_Kitab_At-Tawheed` | كتاب التوحيد | Kitab At-Tawheed (The Book of Monotheism) |

## 🗂 Organization

Each subject directory contains two standardized subfolders:
- `Audio/`: Lesson audio recordings (`.mp3`).
- `PDF/`: Study materials, texts, lesson transcripts, and summaries (`.pdf`).
